TRACE — Tracking and Real-time Analytics for Cargo and Enterprises

IEEE publication · first author

Final-year industry-collaborative project with Conway Packaging Services (CPS) Ltd.

  • Designed and built the tracking device on a LilyGO TTGO T-SIM7000G (ESP32) with BME280 (temperature/humidity) and MPU6050 (accelerometer) sensors, using onboard GPS, 4G and WiFi with MicroSD logging for offline resilience.
  • Prototyped and tested a system to replace CPS's manual shipment-tracking process with real-time visibility, including automated, data-driven alerting to flag damage or misplacement risk in transit.
  • First author on the resulting paper proposing a real-time IoT solution for supply chain traceability, developed with Birmingham City University's College of Computing.
